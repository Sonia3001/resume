Multi-Stage B2B Lead Nurture & Qualification Framework — HubSpot Automation
What I Built
HubSpot automation combining lifecycle stage, ICP fit, engagement scoring, and behavioural triggers. Conditional branches routed contacts into nurture sequences, re-engagement flows, or sales follow-up — with suppression logic and automated lifecycle-stage updates throughout.
Impact
Replaced linear email blasts with intent-based journeys — reducing manual intervention and giving sales clearer visibility into lead intent and funnel stage.
Key Learning
Automation is as much about data governance as workflow design. Clean lifecycle definitions, suppression rules, and regular data-quality audits are what make it scalable.
Lead Scoring & Funnel Visibility — Restructuring MQL / SQL Qualification
What I Built
A qualification framework using ICP fit, lead attributes, lifecycle stage, and behavioural engagement scoring in the CRM — aligned to MQL/SQL progression with funnel reporting to surface drop-off points.
Impact
Shifted focus from lead volume to lead intent — enabling better sales prioritisation and campaign decisions based on funnel performance rather than surface-level engagement metrics.
Key Learning
Fit, behaviour, and lifecycle stage need to work together. High engagement alone doesn't signal quality — the scoring model must reflect both intent and business relevance.
